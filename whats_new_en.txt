🎉 What's New in v1.0.5
• Layer Duplicate: Added the option to duplicate layers easily.
• Layer Opacity: Adjust individual layer transparency for better control over your workflow.
• Color Alpha Slider: Control brush transparency directly within the color picker.
• Two-Finger Undo Fix: Optimized gesture logic to prevent accidental Undo triggers while drawing.
• Social Hub UI: Improved the layout and display of the social media community links.

Previous Updates (v1.0.2 & v1.0.4)
• Panel Data Swipe: Switch between panels instantly by swiping left or right without closing the menu.
• Double-Tap Gestures: Double-tap the left side to Undo and the right side to Redo without breaking your flow.
• Enhanced Burn-ins: New export options to burn in Scene/Panel numbers and Frame Countdowns in MP4.

Core Features (v1.0.0 & v1.0.1)
• Complete storyboard creation with layers and panels.
• Export options for PDF, PNG, MP4, and SGO formats.
• Onion skinning for animation and movement previews.
• Issue-001 Fixed: Resolved project sync issues after .sgo imports.
• Performance: 4096px limit for better stability on all devices.
