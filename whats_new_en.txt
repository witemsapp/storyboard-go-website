🚀 What's New in v1.0.7

• Layer Scaling & Transform: It’s finally here! You can now freely move, scale, and rotate your layers.
• Animatic Dialogues: Display character dialogues directly inside your animatic.
• Lightning-Fast Exports: Rendering is now much, much faster!
• Vector Line Rendering Fix: Improved rendering of lines drawn on the stage—I fixed (I hope) the annoying bug where the vector wasn't being baked to the raster.
• Alpha & Opacity Fixes: Added a dedicated Alpha slider to the color picker. Plus, layer opacity is fully preserved when duplicating panels.
• Smart Panel Add: Adding a new panel now places it directly next to your active one, not at the very end of the project.
• Proper Naming & Numbers: Now, in the Data panel, there is a breakdown by Scene / Shot / Panel.
• Smart Layer Panel Close: Clicking outside the layer panel now automatically closes it to keep your workspace clean. Exception: If you tap a scene thumbnail in the panel strip, the layer window stays open.
• Reddit Community: Added a quick link to our brand-new Reddit community in the "Join Our Community" section!
• The SGO Origin Story: Added a brand new "The Storyboard GO Journey" section in the About tab.
• Official SGO File Icons: Your phone will now proudly display the official SGO icon in your device manager for all .sgo files.
• Custom Loading Icon: Rendering Animatics and PDFs now displays the animated SGO app icon as a sleek loading indicator.
• Clean Duplication: Removed the annoying "copy" suffix from layer names when duplicating entire panels.

🎉 Previous Updates (v1.0.6)
• Focal Zoom: Better focal pinch-to-zoom logic and a new, much higher zoom level!
• SGO Import: Seamless import of SGO files from the file manager and other apps.

• Rendering & Performance: Smoother drawing/erasing experience and improved rendering performance for PDF and MP4 exports.
• Panel Info Scroll: Swiping through panel data now perfectly preserves your scroll position.
