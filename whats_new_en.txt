🚀 What's New in v1.0.6 (First Beta Improvements!)
• Focal Zoom: Better focal pinch-to-zoom logic and a new, much higher zoom level!
• Smart Panel Add: Adding a new panel now places it right next to your active one.
• Duplicate Opacity: Layer opacity is fully preserved when duplicating panels.
• Alpha Slider: Color picker now features an Alpha slider for easier control.
• SGO Import: Seamless import of SGO files from the file manager and other apps.
• Manual Updated: Updated the in-app manual with a new "Import" tab.
• Thumbnails: Improved loading and display of scene thumbnails in the panel strip.
• Drawing & Erasing: Smoother and more reliable drawing and erasing experience.
• Export Rendering: Improved overall rendering performance for PDF and MP4 exports.
• Panel Info Scroll: Swiping through panel data now perfectly preserves your scroll position.
• PDF Panel Names: Fixed an issue where custom panel names/numbers weren't saving correctly in PDFs.
• Clean Duplication: Removed the "copy" suffix from layer names when duplicating entire panels.

🎉 Previous Updates (v1.0.5)
• Layer Duplicate: Added the option to duplicate layers easily.
• Layer Opacity: Adjust individual layer transparency for better control over your workflow.
• Two-Finger Undo Fix: Optimized gesture logic to prevent accidental Undo triggers while drawing.
• Social Hub UI: Improved the layout and display of the social media community links.

Older Updates (v1.0.2 & v1.0.4)
• Panel Data Swipe: Switch between panels instantly by swiping left or right without closing the menu.
• Double-Tap Gestures: Double-tap the left side to Undo and the right side to Redo without breaking your flow.
• Enhanced Burn-ins: New export options to burn in Scene/Panel numbers and Frame Countdowns in MP4.

Core Features (v1.0.0 & v1.0.1)
• Complete storyboard creation with layers and panels.
• Export options for PDF, PNG, MP4, and SGO formats.
• Onion skinning for animation and movement previews.
• Issue-001 Fixed: Resolved project sync issues after .sgo imports.
• Performance: 4096px limit for better stability on all devices.
