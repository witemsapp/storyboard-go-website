🚀 What's New in v1.0.8

• Multi-Track Audio Timeline: A feature so many of you asked for! You can now layer dialogues, music, and sound effects across multiple independent audio tracks and align them with your animatic.
• Volume Keyframes: Craft buttery-smooth fades (fade-in, fade-out) and take precise control over individual clip volume (e.g. ducking music under character voices).
• Audio Clip Tools: Drag, trim, and split audio clips right at the playhead position (Split clip) with a single tap.
• Live Preview Playback: Watch your animatics directly on the canvas with full audio sync—no more waiting for video exports just to check your scene timing!
• Studio Appearance & Decor: A little personal passion project! Deck out your workspace with charming ornaments that physically sway and react to your device’s gyroscope and tilt.
• Smoother Canvas Gestures: Upgraded two-finger canvas rotation—the pivot point now naturally follows your touch focal point, and quick turns no longer accidentally trigger Undo.
• New Export Modals for All Formats: Choose the save directory, customize the number of panels per PDF sheet, scale MP4 and PNG resolutions up or down, and fine-tune many other parameters!
• Audio-Enabled Video & .SGO Export: Export animatics to MP4 with full multi-track audio mixdown, and safely archive your projects in .sgo format with all audio intact.
• Follow for Panels! From now until further notice, follow Storyboard GO on our social media and get a permanent +1 panel boost for each! (Note: this is stored locally on your device rather than linked to a Google account; if you use multiple devices, simply tap follow again and the app will detect it and add +1 to your panel limit).
• Rotation lock when panning across the canvas.
• Easier tool expansion from the drawing toolbar.
• Optimized Landscape Mode: The appearance sheet and audio timeline automatically adapt to landscape orientation.
• Interactive User Manual: Explore brand-new interactive mockups in the user manual demonstrating audio, keyframes, and appearance settings.
• Fresh new look for our website: www.storyboardgo.com!
