3. DATA WE COLLECT
- Local App Data: All your projects, drawings, and metadata are saved locally on your device. We do not store or transmit your projects to our servers.

- Automatically Collected Data: Our app's ad partner (Google AdMob) may collect certain information automatically, including IP address, device ID, advertising IDs, and usage statistics to provide relevant advertisements.

4. LEGAL BASIS FOR PROCESSING (GDPR)
We process your personal data based on the following legal grounds:
- Consent: For beta testing email collection and personalized advertising.
- Legitimate Interest: For providing core app functionality and improving our services.

5. DATA RETENTION
- Beta testing email addresses are retained until you unsubscribe or until the beta testing program ends, whichever comes first.
- Advertising-related data collected by Google AdMob is managed and retained by Google in accordance with their own privacy policy and data retention practices.
- Local app data (your projects) is stored on your device and retained until you delete it.

6. DEVICE PERMISSIONS
To function properly, the app requests the following device permissions:
- Storage / Photos / Media: Required to import background images, open project files (.sgo), and to save/export your projects as PDF, PNG, MP4, or native .sgo files directly to your device storage.
